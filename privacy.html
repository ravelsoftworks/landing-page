Privacy

Last updated 30 September 2026.

Who we are

Ravel Softworks is a software, AI and IT engineering company based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. You can reach us about anything on this page at contact@ravelsoftworks.com.

What you send us

When you use the “Start a project” form, we receive what you type: your name, work email, company, country, the services you pick, and your brief. We use it only to reply to you and to discuss your project. The form is processed by Netlify, which hosts this site.

Analytics cookies

With your permission, we use Google Analytics to understand which pages are useful and how people find us. If you accept, it sets cookies and collects information such as the pages you view, your approximate location and your device type. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set; Google may still receive basic, cookie-free signals (for example that a page was viewed) which it uses to estimate overall traffic. You can change your choice at any time with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of the home page.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

How long we keep it

We keep project enquiries for as long as we are in conversation with you, and for a reasonable period afterwards for our records. Ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing contact@ravelsoftworks.com.
