    Questions buyers ask

    Anything else, ask us directly. We reply within one business day.

    Do you work with companies outside India?

    Yes. We are based in Bengaluru and work with companies in India, the US, the UK and Europe, with working hours that overlap yours for calls and reviews.

    How do you price a project?

    Well-defined projects get a fixed price. Dedicated teams and ongoing support are billed monthly. Either way, you receive a written scope and estimate before any work starts.

    What does AI automation mean in practice?

    Software agents that do a defined piece of work end to end: reading documents and emails, updating your systems, drafting replies or reports, and asking a person when something falls outside the rules you set.

    Is our data safe if we use AI?

    We design around your data policies: business-tier model APIs that do not train on your data, private deployments where needed, access limited to what each workflow requires, and logs of every action an agent takes.

    Can you work with our existing systems?

    Yes. Most of our work connects to software you already use, such as your CRM, ERP, accounting tools, databases and cloud accounts, rather than replacing it.

    Who owns the code?

    You do. Source code, accounts, data and documentation belong to you, and live in your repositories and cloud accounts wherever possible.

    Will you sign an NDA?

    Yes, before you share anything sensitive.

    What happens after launch?

    You choose. We can stay on for monitoring, support and improvements, or hand over to your team with documentation and a walkthrough.
